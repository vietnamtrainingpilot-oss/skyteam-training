## Training Path

**01 - Apply**

Complete the application for the department you want to join.

**02 - Study**

Read the department guide and confirm any airline- or server-specific procedures with a trainer.

**03 - Demonstrate**

Complete the written examination and practical questions.

**04 - Operate**

Receive feedback from a qualified trainer before joining live operations.

## Using The Materials

These guides are shared templates. Adapt them to match the procedures, fleet, routes, ranks, communications, and server rules used by your organization.

Replace placeholders such as `[AIRLINE]`, `[CALLSIGN]`, `[DESTINATION]`, `[ORIGIN]`, `[GATE]`, and `[AIRCRAFT TYPE]` before using scripts or examples in live operations.

Before an examination is issued, trainers should verify that the material is current, the examples match the organization, and all candidates are working from the same revision.

## Standards

Training should promote realism, clear communication, teamwork, consistency, and respectful passenger service. When circumstances change, good judgment matters: realistic recoveries such as a go-around, delayed departure, or revised ground setup are part of good operations.

## Contributions

Corrections, clearer examples, updated procedures, accessibility improvements, and new visual references are welcome.

When contributing, explain what changed and whether it applies across PTC or only to one airline or server. Keep organization-specific material clearly marked so the shared resources remain useful to everyone.
