## Purpose

This handbook provides authorized trainers with guidance for grading examinations and conducting evaluations. The department sections explain how to grade tests and supervise participants.

> **Confidentiality:** This handbook must not be shared outside the authorized training team.

---

## Trainer Development

Trainers must complete the required examinations and evaluations before receiving full trainer authorization. Trainers may request support from a senior trainer when necessary.

For Phase 1, trainers must grade examinations using the guidelines provided for each department. Participant responses should be reviewed using the approved training-record system.

When grading an examination, assess the following:

- **Detailed answers:** Responses should demonstrate sufficient understanding.
- **Grammar and punctuation:** Responses should be clear and understandable.
- **Meaningful answers:** Responses should include relevant examples, reasons, or additional details.
- **Originality and honesty:** Responses must be completed independently. Copying answers or using unauthorized assistance may result in an immediate fail.
