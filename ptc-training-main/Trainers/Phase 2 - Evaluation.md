# Stage 2 Private Evaluation

## Trainer Guide

---

## Purpose

This guide provides trainers with a standardized framework for conducting Stage 2 private evaluations. It is intended for trainer use only and covers evaluation procedures, grading criteria, routes, and pass requirements.

---

## Evaluation Overview

Stage 2 evaluates whether the participant can apply the procedures and skills covered during Stage 1 in a practical environment.

### Trainer Responsibilities

Trainers should:

- Explain the evaluation requirements before beginning.
- Use the grading criteria consistently.
- Provide realistic operating conditions.
- Record performance accurately.
- Give clear feedback after the evaluation.
- Submit the final result through the appropriate staff channel.

A participant must achieve the required pass rate for their department. Participants who do not meet the requirement should be referred for further revision or retraining.

---

## Evaluation Departments

Stage 2 evaluations may be conducted for the following departments:

- Flight Deck
- Cabin Crew
- Ground Crew

Use the grading criteria for the relevant department only.

---

# Flight Deck

**Maximum Score:** 60 points  
**Pass Requirement:** 48 points

| Criterion | Description | Points |
|---|---|---:|
| Pushback | Quality and accuracy of the pushback procedure. | 5 |
| Taxi | Taxi alignment, gear positioning, speed control, and awareness. | 5 |
| Takeoff | Flap and throttle configuration, speed control, performance, and smoothness. | 10 |
| Climb | Climb attitude, climb rate, steepness, and speed control. | 5 |
| Cruise | Aircraft configuration, attitude, and speed during cruise. | 5 |
| Descent | Descent profile, attitude, path, flap configuration, and speed. | 5 |
| Landing | Approach, alignment, touchdown, and landing smoothness. | 20 |
| Taxi to Gate | Taxi alignment, gear positioning, speed control, and gate approach. | 5 |
| **Total** |  | **60** |

## Recommended Evaluation Routes

- Keflavik → Sauthepomea — hill approach
- Tokyo → Perth — Runway 29 approach
- Mellor → Greater Rockford — Runway 7L approach
- Sauthepomea → Keflavik — Runway 16 approach
- Izolrani → St. Barts — beach approach
- Izolrani → Laranca — Runway 26 approach

> Weather conditions are not fixed and may be selected randomly by the trainer.

# Cabin Crew

**Maximum Score:** 55 points  
**Pass Requirement:** 44 points

| Criterion | Description | Points |
|---|---|---:|
| Check-in | Check-in procedure and related public-address announcements. | 10 |
| Boarding | Boarding procedure and related public-address announcements. | 10 |
| Pre-flight Service | Completion of the required pre-flight service procedures. | 5 |
| Safety Demonstration | Safety-related public-address announcements and demonstration. | 4 |
| Takeoff | Required takeoff announcements. | 3 |
| In-flight Service | In-flight service procedures and related announcements. | 10 |
| Descent and Landing | Required descent and landing announcements. | 3 |
| Passenger Situation | Response to a simulated passenger interruption or issue. Assess professionalism, communication, and adherence to procedure. | 10 |
| **Total** |  | **55** |

# Ground Crew

**Maximum Score:** 50 points  
**Pass Requirement:** 40 points

> All Ground Crew evaluations should be conducted at Perth.

| Criterion | Description | Points |
|---|---|---:|
| Cone Placement | Correct placement and positioning of cones. | 6 |
| Aircraft Setup | Completion of the required setup tasks. Award 5 points for each vehicle. | 30 |
| Pushback | Execution, control, safety, and smoothness of the pushback procedure. | 10 |
| Marshalling | Accuracy, timing, communication, and control during marshalling. | 4 |
| **Total** |  | **50** |

---

# Trainer Notes

- Apply the same standards to every participant.
- Award points based on observed performance rather than assumptions.
- Deduct points when a task is incomplete, unsafe, incorrect, or requires excessive assistance.
- Record specific errors to support post-evaluation feedback.
- Do not coach during the evaluation unless intervention is required for safety or server operation.
- Clearly communicate the final result and any required follow-up training.
