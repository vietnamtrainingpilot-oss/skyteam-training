# Cabin Crew Study Guide

## I. Check-In
**Information:** The check-in part will happen at the check-in section of the airport. You will have to sit at a desk and provide a boarding pass for passengers to board the flight. You should ask the passenger about their destination, identification, class, whether they want a digital or normal boarding pass, and their luggage, and finally give a brief overview of the flight and its destination to confirm that their booking details are correct. Must check their role before giving out a boarding pass.

**Play this PA Announcement before starting check-in:**

> Check-in is now open for [AIRLINE] flight [CALLSIGN] to [DESTINATION]. Passengers are requested to proceed to the check-in counter. Please have your passport and travel documents ready. Thank you.

**And this announcement for final check-in:**

> This is the last call for check-in on [AIRLINE] flight [CALLSIGN] to [DESTINATION]. Passengers who have not yet checked in are urgently requested to proceed to the counter immediately. The counter closes in 10 minutes.

### Example Dialogue

- CA: Hello @, welcome to the [AIRLINE] check-in counter at [DEPARTURE AIRPORT]. May I ask for your destination, sir?
- Passenger: I'm going to [DESTINATION].
- CA: Certainly! May I see your passport, please?
- Passenger: Here. Passenger hands pass
- CA takes pass, hands back
- CA: Thank you! Which class are you flying with today, sir?
- Passenger: "I'll go with business class."
- CA: Great choice! Do you want a digital boarding pass or a normal boarding pass, sir?
- Passenger: "I'll go with the digital one, please."
- CA: Okay.
- CA creates digital bp creates QR code for bp hands QR code to passenger (1)
- CA confirms print; prints bp hands BP (2)
- Passenger: Thanks.
- CA: Is there any overweight luggage, sir?
- Passenger: Yes, only this luggage. Passenger places luggage
- CA weighs luggage, scans luggage
- CA: Your flight number will be [CALLSIGN] and operated by an [AIRCRAFT TYPE], and your gate is gate [GATE]. Thank you for choosing [AIRLINE] for flying and have a great flight!
- Passenger: Thank you!

**Notes:** (1) is for the digital one and (2) is for the printed one

## II. Boarding

**Information:** The gate boarding part will happen at the flight gate. You will have to sit at a desk and check all the boarding passes of the passengers of that flight so they can board the flight. You should ask the passenger for their boarding pass and let them board the plane.

### Example Dialogue

- GA: Hello @, welcome to [AIRLINE] flight [CALLSIGN]! May I take a look at your boarding pass, sir?
- Passenger: Sure!
- Passenger gives boarding pass
- GA scans boarding pass, hands back BP
- Passenger: takes
- GA: Thank you! All details are correct, and you may board the plane.
- Passenger: Thank you!
- GA: You're welcome, enjoy your flight!

**Note:** for international flights, please also ask them for their passport. You may ask them however you want, but remember to be professional and formal to passengers.

## III. Preflight Service

At [AIRLINE], we offer business class. The host will tell you if we have business class or not. After all the business class passengers board, you may serve them with preflight service. Follow the dialogue example below:

- You: Hello, welcome to Business Class preflight service. What would you like to have?
- Passenger: I'll take a glass of water.
- You: Sure! hands water
- You: Also, here is your hot towel for being a Business Class Passenger — please call me if you need help!
- Passenger: Thank you!

You may come back to your seat when preflight service is completed.

## IV. Safety Demonstration

**Play this PA Announcement before pushing back — this is an example; replace it with the actual flight details:**

> Ladies and Gentlemen, this is your Captain speaking. Welcome onboard [AIRLINE] Flight [CALLSIGN]. Our estimated flight time for this flight is approximately an unknown number; the flight today is expected to be smooth with no turbulence. We will be starting the safety instructions shortly — please rely on them and ensure that your seatbelts are fastened, your tray tables are stowed, and your window shades are open. If you need any assistance, please contact a crew member in the aircraft in front of or behind you. Thank you for choosing [AIRLINE].

## V. Takeoff PA

> Ladies and gentlemen, we are now cleared for takeoff from [ORIGIN]. Crew is now required to be seated. Please ensure seatbelts are securely fastened, tray tables are stowed, and window shades are open.

## VI. Inflight Service

**Information:** After you get the confirmation from the pilot, you may announce to start the flight service:

> Ladies and gentlemen, we will now begin our in-flight service. Our crew will be coming through the cabin with food and beverages.

Now, you may begin the flight service. You should ask passengers what they want and provide help to passengers if needed. You should ask them what food they want, serve it, ask them if they want anything else, provide them with what they also need (if they asked), and thank them and tell them to enjoy the meal.

### Example Dialogue

- FA: Hello, welcome to [AIRLINE] in-flight food service on flight [CALLSIGN] to [DESTINATION] ([ORIGIN]). May I take your order, please? @
- PAX: Can I have a bowl of [MEAL]?
- FA: Of course! Please wait a moment while I prepare your order. @
- FA: prepares the meal, places food on the table for passenger
- FA: Here is your food. Would you like anything else? @
- PAX: takes food
- PAX: Can I also have a glass of milk also?
- FA: Of course! Your drink will be out shortly. @
- FA: takes out glass and pours drink into glass; hands glass to passenger
- FA: Here is your drink. Enjoy your meal! @
- PAX: Thank you! takes drink

## VII. Landing/Descent
When completing the flight service, the plane will start descent. Play this PA Announcement for descent:

> Ladies and gentlemen, we have begun our descent into [DEST]. Please return your seats and tray tables to the upright position, stow all carry-on items, and ensure your seatbelts are fastened. All electronic devices should remain in flight mode until we have come to a complete stop.

After landing, play this welcome announcement:

> Ladies and gentlemen, on behalf of [AIRLINE] and your flight crew, welcome to [DEST]. Please remain seated with your seatbelts fastened until the aircraft has come to a complete stop and the seatbelt sign has been turned off.

Then, keep waiting for the plane to taxi to the gate.

## VIII. Deboarding
When the plane reaches the gate, you may make this announcement for deplaning instructions. You should also assist members who need help.

> Ladies and gentlemen, the seatbelt sign has been turned off. You may collect your carry-on baggage from the overhead bins. Please take care as items may have shifted. Check that you have all your belongings before leaving. On behalf of [AIRLINE], thank you for flying with us today.

And make this announcement to officially end the flight:

> On behalf of your captain and the entire crew of [AIRLINE] flight [CALLSIGN], we thank you for choosing to fly with us today. It has been our pleasure to serve you. We hope to welcome you on board again soon. Have a wonderful day.
